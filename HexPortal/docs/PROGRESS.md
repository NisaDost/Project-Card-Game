## HUD layout (v4.0)
- Touch targets stay ≥ 48 dp (144 units at the 1080-high reference on a 360–430 dp phone); a small icon gets transparent padding up to that size, and neighbouring targets keep ~8 dp of space between them — https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/7101858
- Frequent controls go where the thumbs rest. In landscape with two hands that means the bottom-left and bottom-right corners: Market/blind draw and End Turn on the right, quests and the Mana/Energy gauges on the left. The top corners hold small read-only gauges (towers, round, timer, opponent) plus pause, which is rarely used — https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2016/09/the-thumb-zone-designing-for-mobile-users/
- The HUD root is inset by `Screen.safeArea` (notch / punch-hole) and re-applied when the safe area or orientation changes. The board fills the middle and is no longer framed by full-height side columns.
